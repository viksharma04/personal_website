# Quotes I Like

## Principles
- Superintending: Overseeing and guiding work with vision and care
- Organizing: Creating structure and order from chaos
- Coordinating: Harmonizing efforts and bringing elements together
- Building: Creating and constructing something meaningful
- Repairing: Fixing what is broken and restoring wholeness
